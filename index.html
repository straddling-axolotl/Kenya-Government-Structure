Core Functions of the 3 Arms of Government

The Executive

Law Implementation
Art. 129-155
  • Executes & Implements Laws: Enforces acts passed by Parliament.
  • National Policy: Formulates and directs national public policy and development programmes.
  • Defense & Security: Commander-in-Chief of Defense Forces (KDF) and national security organs.
  • Foreign Affairs: Represents the republic internationally, negotiates treaties & receives ambassadors.

The Legislature

Parliament (Bicameral)
Art. 93-128
  • Law Making: Enacts national legislation binding across the Republic.
  • Representation: Represents the will of citizens and special interest groups.
  • Public Finance Oversight: Approves national budget, taxes, and allocation to counties.
  • Executive Vetting: Approves presidential nominees for Cabinet, Chief Justice, & Ambassadors.

The Judiciary

Law Interpretation
Art. 159-173
  • Interpretation: Interprets the Constitution and resolves legal ambiguities.
  • Dispute Resolution: Administers justice for civil, criminal, and election petitions.
  • Judicial Review: Can strike down executive acts or parliamentary laws deemed unconstitutional.
  • Independence: Subject only to the Constitution and law, independent of political influence.
General Election Ballot

The 6 Direct Ballot Seats Voted by Citizens

During a Kenyan General Election conducted by the Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC Article 88), registered voters cast six ballot papers to directly elect leadership at national and county levels:

Visit Official IEBC Portal
Ballot 1 - Executive
President of Kenya

Elected nationally as a ticket with the Deputy President.

Ballot 2 - County Executive
County Governor

Elected county-wide (1 per County) to run local government.

Ballot 3 - Senate
County Senator

Elected county-wide to represent and protect county interests in Parliament.

Ballot 4 - National Assembly
County Woman Representative

Elected county-wide (1 per County) to promote gender equity & affirmative action.

Ballot 5 - National Assembly
Constituency MP

Elected by constituency voters (290 total) for legislation & local CDF development.

Ballot 6 - County Assembly
Ward MCA

Elected per electoral ward (1,450 total) for grassroots legislation & county oversight.
